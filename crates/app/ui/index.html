On this desktop the screenshot itself comes from the system's own picker — Screensnips hands you the frame the moment you've framed it. One honest gap: GNOME doesn't offer apps a global-hotkey service here, so for a keyboard shortcut, open your desktop's keyboard settings and bind a key to screensnips --capture=picker (region/window picker) or screensnips --capture=full (instant full screen).

Framing your own region on the canvas, pinning a shot to the desktop, and drag-out to other apps are planned, not present — nothing here pretends otherwise.
